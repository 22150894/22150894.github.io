Contract VS SIM Only
A pay monthly contract phone are contracts that you sign to pay out for your phone over an agreed period of time with including the cost of a new handset with a monthly allowance of data, minutes and texts. People usually go for contracts based off if they want the latest model of a phone but would rather not buy a brand-new phone outright.
Pros of using contract include: Spread the cost of your new handset over a time frame, extra benefits and discounts when you sign up, built up your credit rating the longer you have your contract.
A SIM only plan is just a contract for your SIM card. Instead of getting new phones as part of your contract you get new SIM cards with all of the data, minutes and texts that you require, once you have chosen the plan that you want and got your SIM card all you need to do is just put the SIM card into your phoneand then your phone is ready to go. The amount of data allowance you can get with a SIM only plan depends on your data needs as most companies offer multiple allowances for their plans. You also do not have to worry about upgrading as SIM only plans do not need to do this, everyone has become accustomed to upgrading our phones to the next best model after our contracts have ended however SIM only plans do not make you do this as part of their package, all that the SIM only plan requires you to do is choose a new plan and get a fresh SIM card for your current phone.
There are pros and cons to using SIM only plans however. The pros of using SIM only deals are, lower costs as you are not paying off a new phone entirely every month only paying for a SIM card. Flexibility as they are often available on 30 day rolling contracts which allows you to switch providers or change plans easily. Keep your phone because you are only paying for a SIM card and not for an entirely new phone, which is ideal if you are happy with your current phone while also reducing electronic waste. Now these are good effects of using SIM only however there are some cons to using SIM only plans. You must purchase a phone upfront which can be very costly. You need an unlocked phone already which can be a hassle if you are still on an old contract. You do not get a new phone included with this plan so if the phone breaks then you are responsible for the replacement or repairs.
