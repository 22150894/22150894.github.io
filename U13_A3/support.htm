Why you should take out phone insurance

Roaming abroad with most networks will allow you to use your UK allowance when travelling to EU countries under their roam like home option. Other rest of the world countries do daily charges for every call, text and bill of data you use which can get costly quick. Always check with your Network provider before travelling. With the roam like home option you are given different benefits depending on your provider, for example: O2 allows for no daily charge in the EU, however you are subject to a 25GB data roaming limit, Giffgaff offers up to 5GB of roam like home data for free and after that charges apply, iD Mobile includes EU roaming in their plan allowances and much more. These are useful as it allows you to use your UK/home plan allowances such as minutes, texts, and data while travelling in specific countries, these countries are usually the EU and by getting this roam like home plan it allows for the home plan without having to pay extra within these countries.
